About Myself

Hello There, Web Traveller...

My name is Matti, and this entire series of webpages are dedicated to (somewhat) professionally documenting my web design works. Even before I recently dove into the world of HTML and CSS, I was already drawing and designing. As I hope to major in Graphic Design, I've mainly been learning the in's and out's out Adobe programs. I do like to draw just for fun though, and I dabble in a vast array of subjects. If you are really curious, you can shoot me an email at 'mapschooling@gmail.com'!

The design projects displayed in my portfolio are just as vast. They range from full stories told via connected webpages, to experimental art created purely through coding with JavaScript. You will see some of my interest reoccur, specifically my interest in aircraft. I hope the projects and presentation are at least generally fun to look at - that's my main goal in a lot of my works.

Matti Paradise c. 2025

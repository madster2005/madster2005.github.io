Welcome to my Concept Page!

I hope this page conveys my vision for my stylized webpages coherently. For my wireframe sketches, I kept the general format simple but still engaging. I want my pages to use some more detailed elements, like glass effects, inspired off of the "Fruitger Aero" style. Having thumbnail images in bubbles also ties into this, as I feel it gives a lighthearted flare. I am also trying to use gradients, primarily for background elements.
